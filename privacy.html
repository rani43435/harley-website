Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what the Harley app for Meta Quest ("Harley", "the app") does with your information. Harley is made by Marjy Studios. We collect as little as possible, and we never sell your data.

What Harley processes

  • Your voice and messages. When you talk to Harley, your speech is turned into text and sent to the Harley server so an AI model can write her reply. Harley only listens after you say "Hey Harley" or press Talk.
  • Camera images, only when you ask. If you ask Harley about something you are looking at, one still picture from the headset camera is sent with your question so the AI can answer. Harley does not record video and does not keep these pictures.
  • Approximate location. To answer questions like the weather, Harley guesses your city from your internet connection. We do not use GPS.
  • Room layout. Harley uses Meta's scene data (walls, floor, furniture) on the headset to move around your room. This stays on the headset.

Memories stay on your headset

Facts Harley remembers about you (for example your name or plans) are saved only on your headset. You can delete them at any time in Harley's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Services we use

To work, Harley sends the data above to these services, which process it on our behalf:

  • Supabase (the Harley server)
  • AI model providers: Groq, Google Gemini and Anthropic
  • Wit.ai (Meta) for speech recognition
  • Open-Meteo for weather, and an IP-location service for your approximate city

We do not store your conversations on our server after the reply is sent, and we do not use them to train AI models.

Computer tasks (optional)

If you connect Harley to your own computer, tasks you ask for are passed to Claude Code or Codex (whichever you link) running on that computer under your own account. Nothing is sent to your computer unless you set this up.

Children

Harley is meant for users aged 13 and older.

Your choices

  • You can turn off the microphone or camera permission for Harley in your headset settings.
  • You can delete Harley's memories, or uninstall the app to remove all data on the headset.
  • To ask about or delete any data, contact us below.
