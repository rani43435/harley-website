01 Talk

Say her name. She turns around.

No buttons, no menus. Say "Hey Harley" from anywhere in the room and talk like you would with a friend. She answers out loud and looks things up for you: the news, the weather, last night's score.

"Hey Harley, what's the weather tomorrow?"
02 See

She sees your room, too.

Hold something up and ask about it. Harley looks through the headset with you, so "what is this?" and "read me this" just work.

Poke her, pet her, pinch her, throw her across the couch. She reacts to your real hands.

03 Watch together

Movie night for two.

Open a video in the browser and press Watch together. Harley turns to the screen, flies over and sits right next to you. She stays beside the window, never in front of it.

04 Duel

One mode. Impossible.

A sword appears in your palm. Harley blocks, dodges and strikes back, and every hit she lands costs you health. There is no easy mode. Can you beat her?

  • Sword in your hand
  • Real health
  • No easy mode

Questions

Which headsets does Harley run on?

Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S. She uses passthrough, so she lives in your real room.

Do I need controllers?

No. Everything works with your hands: talking, poking, the menu and the sword duel.

What does she remember?

Small things you tell her, like your name and what you like. They stay on your headset. Say "forget everything about me" and they are gone.

Can she use my computer?

Only if you link Claude Code yourself, and only for tasks you ask for. Claude Code asks you before it changes anything, and you can stop it any time.
